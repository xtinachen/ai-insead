Anthropic Hits $965B Valuation, Overtakes OpenAI

Anthropic closed a $65B Series H at a $965B valuation in late May 2026 — the first time any company has topped OpenAI in private AI valuation — while simultaneously filing confidentially for an IPO and projecting its first-ever operating profit of ~$559M in Q2 2026.

WHAT HAPPENED

Anthropic's $65B Series H, co-led by Altimeter, Sequoia, Dragoneer, and Coatue, pushed its valuation to $965B on the back of $47B annualised revenue — nearly double OpenAI's last verified figure of $24B+.

MBA ANGLE

Anthropic is now the default enterprise coding vendor (Claude Code dominates), the safest IPO play in AI, and the clearest case study in B2B-first AI monetisation — understand their go-to-market before your next interview at any tech, consulting, or PE firm.

Apple's WWDC Bets $1B/Year on Google Gemini for Siri

At Tim Cook's final WWDC keynote (opening today, June 8), Apple unveiled a completely rebuilt Siri powered by a custom 1.2-trillion-parameter Google Gemini model licensed at ~$1B per year, plus an Extensions system letting users choose between ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude.

WHAT HAPPENED

Apple's Siri is now powered by Google Gemini at $1B/year, while a new AI Extensions system opens the iPhone to OpenAI's ChatGPT and Anthropic's Claude — effectively making Apple a distribution layer for all three frontier labs.

MBA ANGLE

The real winner is whoever controls the default — Apple's 1.3B device install base means the AI platform wars are now also a distribution deal business, a key lens for any MBA studying platform strategy or negotiating enterprise AI contracts.

Claude Opus 4.8 vs GPT-5.5: Coding Crown Is Now a Tie

As of June 2026, Claude Opus 4.8 leads the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index (61.4 vs GPT-5.5's 60.2) and tops SWE-bench Verified at 88.6% — just 0.1 points ahead of GPT-5.5 — while Microsoft launched its own MAI-Thinking-1 in-house reasoning model at Build 2026, signalling a three-way race.

WHAT HAPPENED

Claude Opus 4.8 (shipped May 28, $5/$25 per M tokens, 1M context) and GPT-5.5 (April 2026) are functionally tied on coding benchmarks, while Microsoft's MAI-Thinking-1 — built entirely in-house — reaches human preference parity with Sonnet 4.6 in blind evaluations.

MBA ANGLE

Model parity means the enterprise AI battleground shifts from 'which model is best' to pricing, distribution, and switching costs — exactly the kind of strategic moat analysis that MBAs should be running for any software company client or portfolio company.

60% of C-Suites Plan Layoffs for AI Non-Adopters

A 2026 Writer/Workplace Intelligence survey of 2,400 executives and employees found that 92% of C-suites are actively cultivating an 'AI elite,' 60% plan to lay off non-adopters, and AI super-users are 5x more productive and 3x more likely to be promoted — while 79% of organisations still struggle to see significant ROI.

WHAT HAPPENED

Despite 59% of companies investing $1M+ annually in AI, only 29% see significant returns — a divide driven by the gap between individual AI super-users (who save 9 hours/week) and organisations that haven't embedded AI into core workflows.

MBA ANGLE

Becoming an AI super-user is now a career survival skill, not a differentiator — INSEAD grads who can both use AI tools and lead enterprise AI transformation programmes (change management, ROI measurement) will command the largest post-MBA wage premiums.

Suno Raises $400M at $5.4B — AI Music's Biggest Bet Yet

AI music startup Suno closed a $400M Series D led by Bond Capital at a $5.4B valuation — more than doubling its value in seven months — even as UMG and Sony pursue a $500M copyright infringement lawsuit against it.

WHAT HAPPENED

Suno, which generates 7 million songs daily and hit $140M ARR by late 2025, raised $400M from Bond, IVP, USV, Lightspeed and industry insiders, and announced its first licensed AI music model built in partnership with Warner Music Group.

MBA ANGLE

Suno is the clearest live case study in regulatory arbitrage meets platform disruption — the IP litigation outcome will set the template for how AI companies license creative content globally, making it essential reading for media, entertainment, and IP-focused MBAs.

🔧 Prompt of the Week

Rapidly building an investment or competitive thesis on any AI company — perfect for PE/VC case studies, consulting interviews, or pre-meeting prep.

The AI Due Diligence Brief
You are a senior investment analyst at a top-tier growth equity fund. I need a structured due diligence brief on [COMPANY NAME] as a potential investment or competitor. Cover: (1) Business model and revenue mechanics — how do they make money and what drives retention? (2) Competitive moat — what is genuinely defensible vs. commoditised by model improvements? (3) Key risks — model dependency, IP litigation, regulatory exposure, and customer concentration. (4) AI-specific questions — is their advantage in data, distribution, fine-tuning, or workflow integration? (5) 3 smart questions I should ask management in a 30-minute call. Be specific, sceptical, and cite any relevant market data you know. Format as a clean 1-page brief with headers.
💡 Feed it the company's latest press release, funding announcement, or LinkedIn 'About' page as additional context before running — the more specific the input, the more actionable the output.
